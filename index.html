3.How We Use Cookies and Similar Technologies

To ensure the proper functioning of our website, we may sometimes place a small piece of data known as a cookie on your computer or mobile device. A cookie is a text file stored by a web server on a computer or mobile device. The content of a cookie can be retrieved and read only by the server that creates the cookie. Each cookie is unique to your web browser or mobile application. Cookies usually contain identifiers, site names, and some numbers and characters.

Like many other websites and Internet service providers, we use cookies to enhance user experience. Cookies allow websites to remember your single visit (using session cookies) or multiple visits (using permanent cookies). Cookies also allow websites to save settings such as the language, font size, and other browsing preferences on your computer or mobile device. This means a user does not need to reset preferences for every visit. Without cookies, a website would view each visit as a new user, resulting in the user being logged out if they are redirected to another page. PT. Braincode Solution only uses cookies for purposes outlined in this Statement. You can manage or delete cookies based on your preferences. For more information, visit AboutCookies.org. You can clear all cookies stored on your computer, and most web browsers allow you to block cookies. However, this would require adjusting your user settings every time you visit our website. To learn how to manage cookie settings for your browser, please visit the following links:

Internet Explorer Google Chrome Mozilla Firefox Safari Opera
